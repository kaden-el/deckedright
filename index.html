Common questions

Things homeowners ask before submitting.

Is this really free?
Yes. Free to you, always. The deck contractor pays DeckedRight a small fee when we send them a pre-qualified homeowner. You owe us nothing, ever — not for the introduction, not for the project, not later.
What does "vetted" actually mean?
Every contractor in our network is licensed, insured, and has been doing deck work in West Michigan for at least three years. We've reviewed their portfolio, their references, and their reviews before they got a spot. We cap the network at three contractors per metro on purpose — every project gets real attention.
What if I don't like the contractor you connect me with?
You're under zero obligation. If the first contractor isn't the right fit — wrong style, didn't show up on time, anything — text us. We'll loop in another one from the network. If the network still isn't right for your project, we'll point you elsewhere and the whole thing costs you nothing.
How fast will I hear back?
From us: 1–2 hours during business hours (Mon–Sat 8am–8pm ET). From the contractor: usually under 4 hours, often under 1. Speed-to-lead is the biggest factor in close rate, and our contractors know it.
Do you work in my area?
We cover Kent County and the adjacent counties: Ottawa, Allegan, Ionia, Barry, and Montcalm. If you're outside that radius, we'll tell you on the qualification call and point you to a contractor who covers your area.
What kinds of decks do your contractors build?
New builds, full replacements, and repairs across pressure-treated, cedar, composite (Trex, TimberTech, Fiberon), and PVC. From a 12×16 starter deck to a multi-level outdoor living buildout with lighting and railings.
Can I see contractor reviews before I submit?
We don't pre-publish our network members publicly — it's part of how we keep the network small and how we control which contractor your specific project routes to. Once you submit and we qualify the project, we tell you exactly who we're sending it to, and you can look them up before they call.
Why "DeckedRight"?
Because every homeowner we've talked to has the same nightmare story — they hired a contractor on a referral or a Google ad and got burned. We exist for the homeowners who'd rather get one good introduction than ten bad bids. Decks built right, the first time.
